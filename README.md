# HRAnalyticsDashboard
This is an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built to track workforce metrics and employee attrition trends across the organization. It provides a real-time snapshot of headcount , attrition ,and demographic breakdown ,with filters by age group and department.

## Key Metrics
   Total Employees , Active Employees , Attrition Count , and Attrition Rate %
   Average Age and Average Experience across the workforce

## Insights & Visualizations
   ** Attrition by Department ** -- breakdown across Administration, Sales , Operations, IT , Human Resources , Marketing ,and Finance
   ** Attrition by Salary Salab ** -- comparing attrition across 0-3, 3-6, 6-10,and 10+ LPA bands
   ** Attrition by Job Role and Satisfaction** -- cross-tab of job roles against satisfaction ratings (1-4)
   ** Age Group Distribution ** -- employee counts across 18-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-55,and 55+
   ** Attrition by Gender ** -- male vs. female attrition split
   ** Attrition Trend by Experience ** -- attrition patterns across yearsof total experience
   ** Department -Wise Employee Count ** -- headcount by department (Operations, Administration, Sales, IT, Marketing, HR, Finance)


   Built for HR teams and leadership to quickly identify attrition drivers, monitor workforce composition, and support data-driven retention strategies.
